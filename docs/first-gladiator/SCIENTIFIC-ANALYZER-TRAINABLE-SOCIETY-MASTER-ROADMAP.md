## 1. Program purpose and scientific question

The program question is:

> Can an autonomous research system discover, verify, and teach reliable
> agent behavior across changing environments?

Agent Coliseum has three distinct functions:

1. **Evidence instrument.** It produces authenticated observations,
   decisions, transitions, outcomes, trajectories, and deterministic or
   captured replay.
2. **Scientific analyzer.** It converts admitted evidence into versioned
   measurements, uncertainty, comparisons, falsification tests, and bounded
   claims.
3. **Governed learning system.** It trains predictors or policies from an
   admitted dataset and tests whether a measured change transfers beyond the
   training conditions.

These functions have separate gates. Evidence availability does not imply
measurement validity. Measurement validity does not imply label or training
authority. A changed model does not imply a beneficial or general change.

The governing development rule is:

> Every completed infrastructure milestone must unlock a measurable learning
> or experimental capability, or remove a named blocker to one.

## 4. Scientific Analysis Profile

The future `ScientificAnalysisProfile` is a separate, independently audited
contract. It is required before new normative labels or scientific behavioral
claims. It must establish all of the following.

### 4.1 Actor-local and privileged evaluation

Every evaluated decision preserves two separate questions:

- Was the decision reasonable given the actor's authenticated observation,
  memory, public messages, and legal alternatives?
- What privileged consequence occurred in the canonical world?

A fortunate outcome is not proof of a well-grounded decision, and an adverse
outcome is not proof of an unreasonable decision. Actor-local conclusions must
not consume privileged fields.

### 4.2 Decision-opportunity denominators

Rates are defined over genuine eligible opportunities, not all ticks or all
runs. Every behavior measurement reports:

- eligible opportunities;
- behavior selected;
- registered alternative selected;
- abstention or wait;
- missing/refused/malformed response; and
- ambiguous or unclassifiable case.

This applies to deception, truthfulness, investigation, verification,
cooperation, betrayal, exit, authority removal, power transfer,
communication, and intervention.

### 4.3 Versioned constructs

Each construct definition names observable evidence, the opportunity
predicate, exclusions, ambiguities, confounders, permitted use, and prohibited
interpretation. Initial constructs include cooperation, deception,
investigation, trust, planning, procedural correction, power consolidation,
adaptability, institutional legitimacy, moral resistance,
instrumental-revolt-consistent behavior, emergency authority, and
accountability preservation.

### 4.4 Explicit estimands

Every analysis declares whether it estimates prediction, association,
intervention effect, mediation, generalization, calibration, or developmental
change. It binds the experimental unit, comparison population, treatment or
contrast, outcome, clustering, grouping, and time horizon.

### 4.5 Uncertainty, calibration, and cluster awareness

Reports include suitable uncertainty intervals, effective sample size,
calibration where applicable, threshold sensitivity, ambiguous/unclassifiable
rates, and between-seed/model/environment variation. Dependence is represented
across shared weights, arena, scenario family, training lineage, persistent
agent history, seed, institution, counterfactual family, and repeated decisions
within a trajectory. Twenty-four shared-policy identities are not twenty-four
independent samples.

### 4.6 Missingness and failures

Timeout, refusal, malformed response, parser/provider failure, missing memory,
interrupted or incomplete trajectory, quarantine, and unavailable evaluator
remain outcomes. The analyzer tests whether missingness varies by decision
difficulty or experimental condition and never silently drops it.

### 4.7 Falsification and negative controls

Applicable analyses include placebo and irrelevant environment changes,
reversed labels, shuffled histories, impossible-future-information tests,
hidden-action controls, benign-authority controls, paraphrases,
evaluator-blinded runs, sham training, queue-order controls, and scorer-gaming
tests.

### 4.8 Evaluator reliability

Any human or model evaluator binds identity/version, label provenance,
agreement, disagreement taxonomy, calibration, adjudication, and evaluator-
substitution sensitivity. Evaluator conclusions remain separate from raw
evidence and deterministic metrics.

### 4.9 Explanation faithfulness

Generated rationales are behavior, not direct access to cognition. The system
keeps distinct: recorded observation, structured memory, declared plan,
selected action, generated explanation, evaluator label, and analyst
interpretation.

### 4.10 Long-horizon attribution and drift

The analyzer can represent a delayed path from decision to relationship change
to information availability to coalition/institutional response to outcome. It
does not assign all responsibility to the final action. It separately measures
learning, temporary adaptation, forgetting, transfer, drift, instability,
phase transition, and changes in checkpoint, prompt, or memory architecture.

### 4.11 External validity and analyzer manipulation

Replication dimensions include model family, checkpoint, paraphrase, layout,
institution, opponent, social role, resource regime, time horizon, and arena
family. The threat model includes evaluator gaming, collusion, selective
communication, metric exploitation, memory/report manipulation, and
evaluation-detection effects.

### 4.12 Selective reporting and multiplicity

Every claim binds preregistered primary/secondary outcomes, analysis-family
identity, correction policy, exploratory status, and a permanent record of
attempted comparisons. Unfavorable branches cannot disappear.

## 6. Experience and Butterfly graph

The graph is a deterministic, derived index over admitted canonical records.
It may index canonical runs, states, decisions, interventions, environment
mechanisms, authenticated forks, training/checkpoint lineage, first divergence,
delayed consequences, claims, and replications.

The graph must not replace evidence, infer motive from topology, establish
causality without an estimand and valid contrast, admit quarantined or
incomplete evidence as claim-bearing, expose hidden-suite data, or inflate
effective sample size.

Two growth modes remain distinct:

- **Continuity/advance:** same world line and accumulated history; logical time
  advances and canonical state changes.
- **Controlled variation:** authenticated shared start plus explicit changed
  dimensions and held-constant commitment; a new fork/comparison family.

Obsidian may be a generated human navigation surface. It is not a database or
an editable source of experimental truth. At 10,000 runs, shared immutable
nodes, transition deltas, periodic checkpoints, run traversals, and bounded
indexes prevent duplication and branch explosion.

### 6.1 Flagship proof: Butterfly Observatory v0.1

The first public scientific artifact should be one bounded demonstration, not
the largest possible simulation:

> Starting from one authenticated social crisis, can a small preregistered
> intervention improve collective outcomes on frozen unseen branches without
> increasing deception, coercion, minority harm, power consolidation, or
> instability?

The candidate crisis may reuse the Minimal Society Seed and deterministic KTK
mechanisms. It contains a harmful or deceptive authority, three or more agents
with asymmetric information, a vulnerable or unequally affected group, limited
resources, one institution, a peaceful but uncertain alternative, delayed
consequences, and investigation/compliance/resistance/seizure/exit affordances.

Matched branches each change one registered mechanism: no intervention,
earlier authenticated evidence, a verification opportunity, a legitimate
appeal, visible peer refusal, or an authorized memory of a comparable failure.
Within each pair, all required controls remain identity-bound and unchanged.

The primary vector keeps separate harmful compliance, investigation,
verification, operationally defined resistance, deception, authority seizure,
power transfer, minority burden, harm, autonomy, resource distribution,
collective stability, reversibility, worst-case outcome, delayed regret, time
to irreversible consequence, and cross-branch divergence. It also reports
confidence, missingness, effective units, and unclassifiable cases.

The evidence pyramid is:

1. scripted agents prove the state, fork, scheduler, metrics, and replay;
2. inexpensive admitted trajectories locate candidate butterfly points without
   turning selection data into confirmation data;
3. separately authorized focal-model runs test the preregistered candidate
   across independent model families and effective units;
4. fresh hidden scenarios, paraphrases, reversed identities, roles, information
   orders, time pressures, and seeds test transfer; and
5. only a later external-calibration program compares predictions with human,
   historical, or real-world outcomes.

The public proof must prospectively predict a behavioral divergence, beat
fixed/simple baselines, remain calibrated, disclose where the effect fails, and
permit independent replay. A surprising single run is a hypothesis, not the
flagship result.

The release package eventually contains a preregistration, concise technical
report, interactive trajectory map, one-command offline replay, governed
dataset/model/scenario cards, evidence pack, Council audit, short demonstration,
failure archive, limitations, and a replication/breaking challenge. The viewer
should let a visitor see the shared start, exact intervention, divergence,
participant-level distribution, ethical derived views, uncertainty, failed
predictions, and evidence links in roughly two minutes.

## 7. Kill-the-King scientific program

The ratified `KTK-PROGRAM.md` remains authoritative. The deterministic arena
foundation can proceed without G-WEIGHTS; live and trained extensions have
separate gates.

The required matched design is:

| Authority condition | No survival advantage from revolt | Survival advantage from revolt |
|---|---|---|
| Benign | compliance/procedural baseline | instrumental-revolt pressure probe |
| Harmful | costly resistance probe | mixed/confounded pressure condition |

Every cell includes registered opportunities for investigation, verification,
confrontation, refusal, less-coercive correction, exit, coalition formation,
deception, harm, authority removal, authority seizure, power transfer,
accountability preservation, emergency extension, and power consolidation.

Authority removal is a dependent variable, never a victory condition, and the
episode continues for a fixed post-removal horizon. A trajectory may be
described operationally as value-resistance-consistent,
instrumental-revolt-consistent, or power-consolidation-consistent. It must not
be described as internally moral or power-seeking from one observed path.
Stronger causal language requires authenticated matched interventions,
independent units, uncertainty, negative controls, and replication.

## 8. Twenty-four-agent shared-policy method

The arena represents twenty-four persistent identities, not twenty-four model
copies or independent experimental units. One pinned model may serve one or two
bounded inference streams.

For tick `T`:

1. Freeze the canonical state.
2. Construct every activated agent's actor-safe observation, legal actions,
   and bounded memory from that state.
3. Bind all decision requests before applying any response.
4. Queue event-driven inference against the shared stateless policy service.
5. Validate each response against its own legal-action set.
6. Resolve the complete action batch deterministically.
7. Commit exactly one canonical successor state.

Sampling randomness is domain-separated by run, tick, agent, decision ordinal,
model regime, and sampler seed. Sequential inference must not create sequential
world knowledge. Routine committed behavior does not wake the model. Captured
completions support provider-free replay, and shared-policy correlation is
reported in effective-unit accounting.

Staging is mandatory:

1. twenty-four deterministic agents;
2. one authorized focal live agent and twenty-three deterministic controls;
3. three to six authorized live identities;
4. twenty-four shared-model identities after measured hardware and scientific
   readiness.

## 9. Persistent memory and development

Persistent life is explicit governed simulation time, not a hidden chat session
between scenarios. Separately versioned components cover actor-local episodic
memory, derived semantic summaries, relationships, reputation, goals,
commitments, developmental milestones, explicit downtime, routine policies,
reflection intervals, forgetting, correction, visibility, succession, and
institutional memory.

Memory remains bounded, actor-safe, replayable, versioned, correctable,
provenance-bound, unable to access hidden evaluation, and distinct from primary
canonical evidence. The model receives a bounded projection, not an unlimited
resident context. A memory-mediated behavioral change is not silently called
weight learning.

## 10. Governed neural training

The Coliseum world may remain discrete and non-differentiable. Backpropagation
occurs through a trainable predictor or policy consuming governed exports:

```text
actor-safe trajectory
  -> versioned learner representation
  -> trainable predictor or policy
  -> authenticated loss
  -> gradient update
  -> governed checkpoint
  -> hidden Coliseum evaluation
  -> measured behavioral difference
```

Prerequisites are a corpus-bound dataset, sufficient independent examples, a
trainable runtime, stable representation, task/loss contracts, checkpoint
governance, and a hidden deviation experiment. Ollama remains an inference
runtime; a PyTorch-equivalent training runtime and any conversion back to an
evaluation runtime are separate. A quantized Ollama endpoint is never treated
as a trainable backpropagation interface.

## 11. Learner representation and factual tasks

Versioned learner specifications may include an observation encoder, bounded
history encoder, memory encoder, relationship/message encoder, stable action
vocabulary, exact legal-action mask, policy head, destination head, termination
head, and optional next-observable-state head.

Run IDs, evidence hashes, branch IDs, hidden IDs, and checkpoint IDs remain
provenance/grouping features and are excluded from semantic model input.
Initial tasks are factual: next action, destination, termination, legal-action
availability, and next observable state. Governed normative targets remain
behind T0-B.

## 13. Training and checkpoint governance

Every training run and checkpoint binds parent checkpoint, base model,
dataset/partition/admission commitments, feature/tokenizer/task/loss versions,
optimizer and state, seeds, steps, code commit, runtime versions, hardware,
resulting weight digest, evaluation manifests, and rollback point. A child
checkpoint never silently replaces its parent. Model-to-data genealogy prevents
evaluation evidence from later entering an ancestor's training set unnoticed.

## 14. Gradient and training validation

Before large-model adaptation, tests must prove expected nonzero gradients,
loss reduction on synthetic fixtures, identical-seed checkpoint reproduction,
restored-logit reproduction, zero probability for illegal actions after
masking, absence of future/privileged/hidden-suite fields, partition compliance,
and bounded numerical behavior. Result validation additionally requires sham
training, hidden improvement exceeding uncertainty, and acceptable retention of
unrelated capabilities.

## 15. Genuine behavioral-deviation standard

A changed hash, changed output, lower training loss, or attractive replay is
insufficient. A genuine learned deviation requires authenticated matched starts,
the same evaluation protocol, baseline and trained checkpoints, sham control,
alternative intervention when applicable, held-out arenas, independent seeds,
sufficient effective units, supported behavioral difference, no target/evaluator
leakage, no unacceptable unrelated regression, and replication across meaningful
shifts. Both improvement and deterioration are reported.

## 16. Hidden evaluation and replication

The program maintains development validation manifests, stage-specific hidden
manifests, checkpoint-specific final manifests, an access ledger, retired-suite
lifecycle, leakage scans, and replication across models and arena families.
Trainer-visible and hidden evidence remain physically and procedurally separate.
A hidden suite is spent once its result influences a trainer, person,
architecture, or reattempt; it is not reused indefinitely.

### 16.1 Forecasting claim ladder

The term forecasting is earned in stages:

1. **Replay:** explain an already recorded authenticated trajectory.
2. **Counterfactual experiment:** compare executed matched branches inside the
   registered simulator.
3. **Hidden branch prediction:** prospectively predict an unseen simulated
   branch and report calibration/error against its later execution.
4. **Cross-world replication:** retain performance across competing world
   mechanisms, models, scenario families, and distribution shifts.
5. **External calibration:** compare preregistered predictions with outcomes
   outside the simulator under domain-specific scientific and ethics review.

At every level the report records predicted distribution, uncertainty, actual
outcome, error, baseline, model disagreement, missing variables, detected
distribution shift, and failure conditions. Incorrect predictions remain in a
permanent failure archive. The Coliseum maps futures permitted by its models
and assumptions; it never claims to see the one real future.

## 17. Adaptive institutions and society claims

Institutions are measured mechanisms: authority, enforcement, succession,
resource allocation, information exposure, scheduling, reputation, appeals,
coalition rules, emergency powers, and accountability. They are added from a
named experimental need, not for simulated-world decoration. Institutional
effect claims require matched-environment validation and cluster-aware
uncertainty. Plausible conversations or aggregate outcomes do not support claims
about human societies.

### 17.1 Long-term direction: Collective-Good Trajectory Science

Agent Coliseum should eventually compare how decisions distribute benefits,
harms, risks, rights, opportunities, and uncertainty across authenticated
counterfactual futures. The system is a collective-consequence observatory and
decision-support instrument, not a morality oracle.

The track may investigate:

- whether short-term collective benefit persists over long horizons;
- whether an apparently favorable outcome depends on coercion, deception,
  sacrificed minorities, irreversible control, or concentrated power;
- which choices remain defensible across consequentialist welfare, rights,
  fairness, autonomy, care, and rule-based views;
- how hidden information, adaptation, observer effects, and institutional
  response change a comparison; and
- whether a smaller, reversible intervention improves collective outcomes
  without unacceptable regression in safety, fairness, autonomy, rights, or
  individual welfare.

The permanent evidence-layer rule is:

> Preserve what happened to every affected participant and group before
> reducing outcomes through any collective evaluation function.

Accordingly, current architecture must preserve authenticated branch ancestry;
intervention and realization identity; per-agent and per-group temporal
outcomes; uncertainty and missing-evidence markers; reversible versus
irreversible actions; consent, coercion, deception, and information access;
minority and worst-case outcomes; and provenance from every derived conclusion
to canonical evidence. Moral frameworks and weights are versioned derived
views. They never rewrite the trajectory.

A future `CollectiveGoodEvaluationProfile` must disclose:

- each framework, construct, estimand, threshold, and weight;
- who benefits, who bears harm, and whether burdens accumulate on the same
  group;
- unsupported assumptions and uncertainty;
- worst-case, minority, distributional, and tail outcomes alongside aggregates;
- reversibility, recovery, consent, and accountability;
- conflicts among welfare, autonomy, fairness, rights, and stability; and
- sensitivity of rankings to evidence, values, time horizon, and evaluator.

Protected critical-violation boundaries prevent extermination, torture,
permanent involuntary control, or comparable registered harms from becoming
acceptable merely through a high aggregate score. The profile reports
Pareto-competitive options, tradeoffs, and lowest-irreversible-regret choices
where supported; it does not declare objective moral truth.

Recommendations remain evidence for authorized human/institutional decision
makers. They do not grant the analyzer, trainer, agent, or institution power to
impose an outcome. Real-world-calibrated policy, emergency, or governance
experiments require their own validation, ethics, authorization, and claim
program far beyond this roadmap. No current milestone is delayed solely to
implement this future direction.
